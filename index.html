Music

A vibe, not a single sound.

Oliver started DJing at 15 on vinyl, playing house and hard house in Bournemouth, UK. More than 25 years later, he curates a vibe, not a specific sound. Each set is built for the time and the energy of the dance floor.

His passion is reading the room: responding to the crowd and taking the dancers on a journey. Sometimes the sound is upbeat and playful, sometimes dark and mysterious. It's always about the energy.
